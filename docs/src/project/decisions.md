# Decisions

## Settled

- The repository root is the primary sans-I/O Rust core package.
- Android uses a native Kotlin/Compose user interface.
- Android uses the application ID and source namespace `net.fstab.dosegoose`,
  with API 26 as its initial minimum supported version.
- iOS uses a native Swift/SwiftUI user interface.
- Mobile bindings are isolated from the core.
- The desktop simulator uses egui/eframe.
- Every graphical shell follows the system light/dark theme by default and
  offers an explicit light or dark override. Theme-specific colors must preserve
  the same behavior and information hierarchy.
- The project is licensed under `MIT OR Apache-2.0`.
- No project-operated backend is required for reminder operation or backup.
- Durable domain data uses a platform-owned SQLite database behind repository
  interfaces; the database file is not a portable interchange or sync format.
- Android uses Room for medication and schedule records and Preferences
  DataStore for small application preferences such as theme mode.
- Android system back and visible Back controls resolve through the same policy:
  dismiss the innermost transient surface, return non-start top-level
  destinations to Today, and leave the app only from an unobstructed Today.
- Medication and schedule identifiers are UUID strings generated by the native
  shell. Their identities are independent so later recurrence and sync models
  do not have to treat a schedule as part of a medication's identity.
- Android Today statuses are projections of persisted portable core snapshots,
  not fields on medication configuration.
- Portable snapshots use an explicit versioned JSON envelope. Native databases
  store the snapshot opaquely and may cache only projections produced by the
  core.
- Android deterministically derives a dated occurrence UUID from a schedule UUID
  and local date. Occurrence identity and policy are immutable once materialized;
  schedule edits affect future occurrences.
- Android retains occurrence history independently of configuration foreign-key
  deletion.
- Replacement occurrence state and ordered requested effects are committed in
  one Room transaction. Effects remain in a pending outbox until Android
  reconciles the requested platform state and acknowledges them.
- Android alert reconciliation is idempotent and state-based so upgrading an
  existing installation cannot replay its historical effect backlog. Regular
  due and intrusive presentations share one deterministic occurrence-scoped
  notification identity. Switching phase replaces the card rather than stacking
  a second card; the old intrusive identity is cancelled on upgrade.
- Android maps the portable core's non-intrusive `Quiet` presentation to a
  normal default-importance due notification. A distinct silent or minimized
  due-notification mode is not part of the initial behavior.
- An alert synchronization snapshot carries an effect-ID cutoff, and Android
  acknowledges only effects through that cutoff after successful platform
  work. Concurrently created effects remain pending for replay.
- Medication notification details stay on the phone: lock screens receive a
  generic public version and Android companion-device bridging is disabled.
- The intrusive action is **Snooze 10 min** when ten minutes fit inside the
  inclusive late-dose window, then **Snooze until [cutoff]**. The latter uses
  the absolute deadline; it does not round a duration past eligibility.
- An intrusive notification is re-alerted on a fixed ten-minute cadence after
  the core first requests intrusive presentation. The durable effect timestamp
  anchors that cadence; one-shot Android alarms recover it after process death
  or reboot. Ordinary state refreshes are silent. Expiration, intake, reset to
  inactivity, and snooze cancel the pending re-alert. A stale alarm is checked
  against current occurrence state before it may sound.
- Notifications have no dismiss action and are marked ongoing. System removal
  of a card restores it silently and does not change dose state or cancel its
  durable reminder schedule; Android may still allow removal of ongoing cards.
  Legacy notification dismiss intents restore state rather than dismiss the
  core presentation.
- Snooze never marks intake or resets accepted activity. A snooze ending exactly
  at the late cutoff does not sound a one-second alarm immediately before
  expiration. Notification expansion is not treated as a dose action.
- Android uses user-granted `SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM` access when available and an
  inexact idle-capable alarm otherwise. It schedules a durable midnight
  reconciliation as well as portable-core evaluation boundaries.
- A newly created, re-enabled, or changed schedule is effective at the explicit
  foreground action time. A scheduled occurrence earlier than that boundary is
  excluded; the initial conservative policy begins with the next occurrence.
- Schedules migrated from database schema 2 retain an effective boundary of
  zero so an existing user's active schedule continues without being treated as
  newly created. A still-open overdue occurrence may therefore alert after the
  upgrade; historical effect rows are not replayed directly.
- Android reconciles the previous, current, and next local dates on startup and
  foreground entry so cross-midnight early windows are materialized, and
  schedules an in-process reconciliation for the next known policy boundary or
  local midnight.
- The initial once-daily Today projection may carry yesterday's occurrence
  across midnight only while it remains recordable and the current occurrence's
  early window has not opened. A newer occurrence becoming actionable removes
  the prior occurrence from Today; this also permits tomorrow's cross-midnight
  early window to replace today's occurrence. Persistence and reconciliation
  retain both.
- For a daylight-saving gap, Android advances a nonexistent local schedule time
  by the gap. For an overlap, it uses the earlier offset. The resolved zone and
  offset are retained on the occurrence.
- Portable domain identifiers distinguish a medication from one scheduled dose
  occurrence.
- The first recurring schedule type is once daily at one user-selected local
  clock time, one early-taking duration, and one late-taking duration. A
  schedule definition and each generated dose occurrence have distinct
  identities.
- The first slice uses absolute Unix-second timestamps supplied by a shell. It
  does not perform calendar or time-zone calculations.
- A late-dose deadline is inclusive. Recording remains available through the
  deadline. A snooze must begin before expiration, end after the action time,
  and end no later than the deadline.
- The early-taking boundary is inclusive. Quiet presentation, recording, and
  activity-gated intrusive presentation begin there. An intrusive reminder may
  also be snoozed during this early interval.
- Ordinary custom intake must be between the early-taking boundary and the
  explicit action time. A distinct, unlocked-foreground override action can
  record an explicitly selected earlier intake before that boundary and marks
  the occurrence taken with override provenance. The selected time cannot be
  in the future, and an elapsed late-dose window still blocks either action.
- Resetting accepted activity is an unlocked foreground action and cancels any
  current intrusive presentation while leaving quiet presentation intact.
- Dismissal only reports presentation state. A later evaluation may request the
  presentation again; only an authorized record action creates intake data.
- Reminder presentation and effects are device-local and carry a device
  identifier. They are not global facts to merge across devices.
- Initialization requests both the early-taking and due-time boundaries when
  distinct. Reconciliation also requests the first instant after the late-dose
  deadline.
- Each device projection rejects events older than its latest accepted event.
  Cross-device merge ordering remains a separate concern.
- Late-window expiration and its cancellation effects are retained even when an
  event's requested action is rejected. This prevents a rejected late action
  from allowing an older action to reopen an elapsed window.
- Accepted activity is ignored after intake is recorded or the late window has
  elapsed. Accepted activity already projected into an occurrence remains until
  an explicit authorized foreground reset; terminal status alone does not imply
  inactivity. Android may separately clear its device-wide activity latch after
  confirmed overnight sleep so later occurrences do not inherit stale activity.
- Android obtains initial activity evidence from the Google Play services
  Activity Recognition Transition API rather than continuously sampling raw
  sensors or running a foreground service.
- Android accepts only a latest `WALKING` or `RUNNING` enter transition, and
  applies it only to enabled, recordable, still-inactive occurrences whose
  early-taking window has opened.
  Exit transitions never reset portable activity state.
- Android activity recognition is demand-driven and gracefully degrades to
  regular due reminders when permission or compatible Google Play services are
  unavailable. Raw activity events are not retained.
- Android uses classification-only Google Play services Sleep API events while
  the device activity latch is active. The initial sleep policy requires two
  hours of high-confidence evidence inside a configurable overnight window
  (default 20:00 through 12:00), tolerates at most 30 minutes between qualifying
  classifications, and resets on strong awake evidence. It intentionally does
  not handle daytime naps.
- Confirmed sleep clears only the Android device activity latch and never
  rewrites an already-active portable occurrence. A subsequent walking/running
  transition clears any unfinished sleep candidate and can activate later
  actionable occurrences. Sleep evidence at or before the latest accepted
  activity is stale and cannot clear the latch. Only policy progress and a
  processed-event watermark are retained; raw sleep classifications are not
  stored.
- Sleep API registration changes are serialized around the asynchronous Google
  Play services tasks. Changing the overnight window invalidates an unfinished
  candidate. A wall-clock correction resets sleep ordering and rebases an active
  latch; a time-zone change resets only the local-window candidate.

## Provisional

- UniFFI is the preferred Kotlin and Swift binding generator.
- User-selected document providers are the preferred cloud-backup mechanism.
- Cross-device synchronization will use mergeable portable state rather than
  a last-writer-wins database file.
- iOS is expected to use SQLite through GRDB and desktop is expected to use
  SQLite through rusqlite, subject to review when those persistence adapters
  are implemented.
- Walking/running-only evidence is the conservative initial Android classifier;
  accessibility needs and field behavior may justify additional evidence types
  later.
